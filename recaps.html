Disrespectful Power Rankings

The Group Chat Edition · Ranked by season strength + recent form. Rankings are editorial, not official standings. Adapted from the supplied weekly write-up; statistics checked against the saved workbook recap.

01 · 400 DEGREEZ

THE LEAGUE LANDLORD

Nine wins, five straight, and the most points in the league. Everybody else is managing a fantasy team; 400 DEGREEZ is collecting rent. At this point, your matchup notification is an eviction notice. The only thing colder than the competition is that 973.04 points-against total. Great team, comfortable schedule, and absolutely no reason to be humble. If this ends without a championship, the group chat is going to turn that 9-1 screenshot into a missing-person poster.

02 · W's in spite of Marv

MARV STILL GETS NO CREDIT

Eight wins and three straight, yet the team name still sounds like an ongoing HR complaint. You beat Run CMC 116.16–102.92, which means the group chat has to tolerate another week of your victory lap. Second place is respectable, but 400 DEGREEZ is upstairs making noise and you still haven't knocked on the door. Keep stacking wins. Marv is apparently the only person in the organization who isn't getting a performance bonus.

03 · The Trade Federation 🏆🏆

A .500 TEAM WITH CHAMPAGNE TASTE

Second-most season points, a 160.38-point week, and somehow still sitting at 5-5. This is a luxury vehicle with the check-engine light permanently on. You destroyed Bi-Polar Boyz by 88.78, but beating that team right now is like winning a boxing match against a coat rack. The two trophies in the name are doing heavy public-relations work. Still, this offense earns the jump to No. 3. Nobody wants you in the playoffs, assuming you remember to qualify.

04 · The Struggle is Real

THE STRUGGLE TOOK PTO

A 150.06-point week is a terrible way to stay committed to the brand. You can't keep calling it a struggle while handing out this much punishment. At 6-4, you're finally looking like a problem instead of somebody explaining why the projections were wrong. One big week doesn't make you royalty, but it does move you ahead of teams surviving on close calls and good fortune. For once, the only people struggling were the ones trying to keep up.

05 · GreenMamba

THE GARDEN HOSE WITH FANGS

Two straight wins, but this week's venom was apparently watered down. Lowest score in a win and narrowest victory: two receipts that collectively mean 'thank God for the opponent.' You edged Pukakke by 7.38 and walked out like you had conquered an empire. At 6-4, you're dangerous enough to matter, but 1,134.60 points against versus 1,106.76 scored suggests the snake has been getting stepped on almost as often as it bites.

06 · Son of a Swan

THE SWAN DIVE

Two straight losses and an 88-point week. That isn't graceful movement; that's a bird hitting a sliding-glass door. Your 6-4 record looks solid until we notice the league-low 958.52 points against. Opponents have been treating your schedule like a wellness retreat, and you're still finding ways to lose. You have the record of a contender and the scoring output of somebody who drafts based on commercials. The cushion is shrinking. Start flapping.

07 · Free R Kelly

ONE WEEK OF WITNESS PROTECTION

League-high 160.82 and a 73.60-point win. For one week, this roster looked like it had stolen the answer key. Suddenly the team with the fewest season points wants a seat at the grown-ups' table. Here's the problem: 160.82 of your 955.74 points arrived this week. We respect the eruption, but one good Sunday doesn't erase nine weeks of loading-screen football. Do it again before printing playoff shirts.

08 · Pukakke

THE HUMAN COIN FLIP

Five wins, five losses, and a Week 10 performance that barely cleared the century mark. You lost to the lowest-scoring winner, which is fantasy football's version of getting fired on your day off. GreenMamba scored 107.82 and still sent you home. Your team name makes an entrance; your roster quietly asks where to park. At .500, you're neither scary nor safely eliminated. You're just standing in the playoff doorway making everybody walk around you.

09 · Catalina Wine Mixer

ALL WINE, NO MIXER

A 96.92-point week while The Struggle is Real dropped 150.06. Your starters hosted a networking event for disappointment. To be fair, opponents have dumped a league-high 1,259.78 on you. That's brutal luck. But this week's 53.14-point loss was not exactly a photo finish. You're getting jumped outside and then coming home to trip over your own furniture. At 4-6, the wine needs to become a lot stronger.

10 · The Hurts Locker

PERFECTLY ORGANIZED DISASTER

An 87.22-point week, a 73.60-point loss, and a front-row seat to DJ's 160.82 eruption. Four straight losses, 3-7, and the group chat has run out of sympathetic emojis. This is a beautifully organized garage sale where every item is broken. The Hurts Locker is living up to the name with a level of commitment nobody asked for. Congratulations on squeezing every last drop out of an empty bottle.

11 · Run CMC

RUN CMC, WALK HOME

You scored 102.92 and still lost by 13.24. Highest points in a loss sounds nicer than 'best seat on the sinking ship.' The rest of the league gets a win column; you get a participation receipt. At 2-8, even a triple-digit week comes with a return label. The team name says Run CMC, but this playoff chase is moving like people who wandered onto the field looking for parking validation.

12 · Bi-Polar Boyz

SAME OLD PAIN

Six straight losses. Seventy-one point six. An 88.78-point beating. The Trade Federation could have spotted you 80 and still won. The league-low score wasn't a close call; it was a full-team commitment to disappointment. At 2-8, the group chat has more material than your lineup had points. The throne is a folding chair outside the championship bracket.
